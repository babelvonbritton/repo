# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Kanban boards with drag and drop (mouse, touch, keyboard), a list view, search, priority filter and sorting.
- Items with summary, label, priority, due date and a note of up to 4000 characters with a live counter.
- Stages and boards can be added, renamed, recolored, reordered and deleted. Deleting a stage moves its items to another stage or to the trash.
- Trash with a fly-to-bin animation, brief info and details per item, restore, and automatic deletion after 7 days, 30 days or never.
- Languages: English (US), Indonesian and Japanese, using next-intl, with default content translated naturally.
- Responsive layout: sidebar on desktop with a sticky footer, floating animated bottom bar on phones.
- Settings: language, boards, menu order, trash keep-time, floating bin and animation switches, privacy and policy card, app and database schema versions.
- Backup and restore as a ZIP with JSON, with checksums, structure validation, visible stages and a merge-style restore that shows differences and lets the user choose.
- One error system for every process, with readable messages and copyable diagnostics.
- Static export with GitHub Pages deployment, MIT license.
